Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how xXSnoogleXx (“we”, “us” or “the developer”) handles information when you play Archaic Sphere (“the Game”). The Game is intended for a general audience, including children.

1. Information stored by the Game

Archaic Sphere stores gameplay information and preferences locally on your device using the device's app/browser storage. This can include game progress, rune and Ascension data, settings, and information needed to calculate offline progress. The Game does not require you to create an account, provide your name, or provide your date of birth.

The Game asks you to select an age range (under 13, 13–17, or 18 or over) so that appropriate advertising and privacy settings can be applied. This age-range selection is stored locally on your device; the Game does not ask for or store your full date of birth.

We do not operate a game account service or our own game-progress server. Gameplay saves are not intentionally transmitted to us by the Game. Local data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game; doing so may permanently remove local progress.

2. Advertising and Google services

The Android version of the Game uses Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) to display banner advertisements. It also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices where required.

When advertising services operate, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app or ad interactions, diagnostics, and information about ad delivery. The exact information and purposes depend on your device, region, applicable law, and the privacy choices available to you. We do not control Google's independent processing of information.

Google's services may use information to provide, measure, limit, and help protect advertising, and where permitted, personalise ads. Archaic Sphere configures Google Mobile Ads with a maximum ad content rating of G and applies age-related advertising treatment based on the age range selected in the Game. Ad requests are subject to Google's consent and eligibility checks; an ad is not displayed when the SDK reports that ads cannot be requested.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Children and families

Archaic Sphere is intended for a general audience, including children. The Game does not ask children to submit personal details such as their name, email address, postal address, or date of birth. The age-range selection is used to configure advertising treatment and is stored locally.

We configure the advertising SDK to use child, teen, or standard age-restricted treatment according to the selected age range, and restrict ad content to a G rating. Google's services may still process device and advertising information as described above, subject to the applicable child and family settings, consent requirements, and laws in the user's region.

Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about privacy in the Game can contact the developer using the details below.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Google's UMP consent flow presents privacy choices before ads are requested. If a privacy-options form is made available, it can be reopened from the Game's privacy options/settings area. Your available choices may depend on your location and applicable requirements.

5. Analytics, accounts and third-party services

The current Game build does not require a player account and does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or advertising server. The third-party advertising and consent services described in this policy are provided by Google. This policy will be updated if the Game adds other services that materially affect information handling.

6. Data security and retention

Gameplay data and preferences are kept in local app storage on your device. We do not receive or retain those local saves on a developer-operated server. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention and security practices, which are described in Google's privacy documentation.
